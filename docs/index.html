RefleK's

Your aim trainer gives scores. RefleK's gives answers.

A free, open-source desktop app that automatically tracks your KovaaK's practice sessions and helps you understand your improvement.

Download for Windows Get Started

Focused features, no clutter

Everything below is available in one streamlined workflow: track, review, compare, improve.

Overview dashboard

See your latest performance, current streak, session progress, and benchmark focus areas in one place.

Benchmarks and progression

Track benchmark tiers, compare targets, and find the next scenario to focus.

In-depth run analysis

Inspect TTK, accuracy, recent score curves, and pinned versus compare overlays.

Mouse movement tracking

Visualize your mouse trajectories, view detailed movement patterns, and understand your crosshair placement over time.

Session overview

Review each session with timeline context, score progression, and scenario breakdowns.
